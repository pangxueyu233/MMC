# Release Checklist

Run these checks before uploading this public package.

- No hard-coded patient/sample identifiers in tracked files.
- No direct identifiers, visit dates, medical record numbers, names, or contact details.
- No private local absolute paths.
- No placeholder manuscript links or empty citation links.
- No exploratory nonlinear association scans in the primary public workflow.
- No patient-specific filtering in figure scripts.
- Cohort filters are field-based and produce participant/sample count summaries.
- All displayed feature/pathway filters are labeled as QC, prespecified display filters, or exploratory.
- All figures are generated by script; no manual figure-editing steps are required.
- Controlled metadata and individual-level derived clinical tables are not committed.
